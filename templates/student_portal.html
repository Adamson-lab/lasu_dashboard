1. Rationale and Philosophy

The philosophy of the BSc in Computer Science programme is to produce graduates well equipped with skills, knowledge and ability that will get them prepared for careers in industries, research and public sectors both nationally and internationally.

2. Aims and Objectives

  • To produce graduates with skills and knowledge that will allow them to be comparable with peers in the international community.
  • To produce graduates who are capable of applying appropriate scientific principles for solving problems for the promotion of human well-being.
  • To teach computer science courses that are relevant to the growing needs of this dynamic world.

3. Admission Requirements

UTME: Five (5) O/Level credits including English Language, Mathematics, Physics to form the core subjects with credit in any other two relevant science subjects.

Direct Entry: Minimum of two "A" level passes or National Diploma Certificate at a minimum of Upper Credit pass.

4. Graduation Requirements

Students entered through UTME must pass courses totaling a minimum of 144 units. Direct Entry students require a minimum of 108 units.
